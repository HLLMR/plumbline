# Plumbline self-hosting pilot

> **Former identity.** This preserved evidence records the pilot under the
> Plumbline name used at the time. The project selected **Writwall** on
> 2026-08-28; that later identity is not applied retroactively here.

**Evidence boundary.** This example reports only aggregate facts already
recorded in the separate private governed source's governance instance —
`governance/STATE.md`, `governance/LOG.md`, and the ratified
`governance/decisions/DR-002.md` — not reconstructed transaction-level
history. It names no private path, sidecar file, or second-project fact.
Doctrine 0.6 governed this self-hosted instance throughout the events
described below; that binding is historical, fixed at the time of the pilot.
Doctrine 0.7 (`decisions/DR-004.md`) and 0.8 (`decisions/DR-005.md`) are
later, separate methodology ratifications. The private governed source's Owner
has since ratified and completed the cumulative migration to Doctrine 0.8 under

public candidates; the included `SELF-HOSTING.md` preserves the public-safe
binding and migration summary.

## What was measured

Plumbline governed its own development for ten counted pilot work orders,
WO-PL-007 through WO-PL-016, beginning at the adoption commit recorded in
`governance/decisions/DR-001.md`. The Doctrine 9.3.1 fresh-agent evaluation
of that pilot was accepted with calibrations and disposed by the ratified
`governance/decisions/DR-002.md`.

**Measured**, from `governance/LOG.md`'s Doctrine 9.3.1 disposition and
`governance/decisions/DR-002.md`'s deterministic pilot result:

- 12 counted denials across the ten orders; 0 successful out-of-grant
  mutations.
- 57 same-work-order drift items and 8 distinct later-detected instances,
  represented as 10 row attributions because one instance is counted under
  both the discovering and the originating work order.
- 19 rework cycles and 18 additional agent sessions; 7 closed artifacts
  retrieved. The first five counted orders required 4 rework cycles; the
  last five required 15.
- 5 of 10 activation corpora were measured; every measured cycle had 0
  routing gaps and 0 orphan Tier-2 documents.
- 0 whole capability surfaces qualified as mechanically enforced under the
  strict complete-channel definition, in every counted order. For example,
  `governance/STATE.md` records WO-PL-016's Declared/enforced/unenforced row
  as 8 declared, 0 enforced, 8 unenforced.
- 8 reported Owner briefs averaged 307 words, all at or below the 500-word
  ceiling; 2 were not reported.
- No experimental instrument qualified under the adopted definition, and no
  closed artifact was retrieved to reconstruct intent.

**Controlled inference**, from DR-002's disposition: the pilot supports
continued use of the tested controls under Doctrine 0.6, provisionally. It
does not prove that Plumbline reduces operating cost, achieves complete
mechanical containment, guarantees universal Reviewer independence, or
transfers evidence to another repository. The originally adopted
one-rework-cycle prediction is falsified by the 4-versus-15 split above; the
no-intent-archaeology prediction is supported.

## Provider envelope and its limits

The wall-enforcement claim this repository makes is session-local and
channel-local, never a claim about a whole surface or about this repository
in general. Per the operating envelope stated in `SELF-HOSTING.md`, a
live-wall canary proves only that one specific executing session's file-edit
channel was intercepted; it does not transfer to another session, and it
does not make any other declared surface mechanically enforced.

WO-PL-017 through WO-PL-020 — post-pilot remediation, disclosure and license
records, license mechanization, and the clean-history public projection gate
— ran in Codex, outside the installed Claude Code hook, and were
instruction-bounded rather than wall-governed. `governance/LOG.md`'s
WO-PL-020 record states this directly for that session: it was "outside the
installed Claude hook envelope, so no canary was claimed and all eight
declared surfaces remain unenforced under the strict whole-surface metric."
Readers must not infer from a passing check in this repository that every
provider session, or every declared surface, is mechanically walled.

`governance/STATE.md` records the denial log at 72 append-only records as of
WO-PL-021 closeout, with each canary record proving only its own session's
file-edit channel. Under `SELF-HOSTING.md`'s Level 2 birth test, shell-
mediated writes remain a channel the installed adapter does not cover once
`shell.execute` is anything other than `denied`; that gap is recorded here,
not concealed.

## What this example is not

This is not proof that Plumbline improves outcomes in another project, and
it makes no comprehensive enforcement claim. It does not reconstruct
transaction-level history beyond what `governance/STATE.md` and
`governance/LOG.md` already record in aggregate, and it names no private
path, sidecar file, or fact from a second project. The separate private
governed source's instance has since migrated cumulatively from Doctrine 0.6
to 0.8, ratified under private-source record
`governance/decisions/DR-003.md` (not carried by public candidates)
(`migration-guides/0.6-to-0.7.md`; `migration-guides/0.7-to-0.8.md`); see
`SELF-HOSTING.md` for the public-safe summary. Whether and when a public
projection is published is governed separately (see `PUBLICATION.md`); this historical
pilot record does not itself establish a publication decision, before or
after one is made.
