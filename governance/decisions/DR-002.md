# DR-002: Plumbline pilot evaluation disposition

**Status:** RATIFIED by the Owner (HLLMR), 2026-08-20
**Scope:** Plumbline repository-development role
**Evidence boundary:** Doctrine 0.6 §9.3.1 fresh-agent evaluation of the ten
counted work orders, followed by Codex verification of the material findings

## Decision

Retain Doctrine 0.6 **provisionally**. The pilot supports continued use of the
tested controls; it does not prove that Plumbline improves outcomes, reduces
cost, governs every provider, or transfers evidence between repositories. No
control meets the §6.5 retirement bar. Publication and licensing remain blocked
until WO-PL-017 closes and the separate chain-of-title decision is made.

The Owner accepts the fresh evaluation with these calibrations:

- provider execution blockage does not automatically require an RFI when the
  work order anticipated it and no grant ambiguity exists;
- deterministic reruns preserve technical evidence but do not alone preserve
  Reviewer independence;
- traversal denials remain real denials while their tool-call ergonomics are
  reported separately;
- the evaluator's aggregate findings are decision-grade, while report-level
  conclusions remain qualified because it did not read most implementation
  reports despite being permitted to do so.

## Deterministic pilot result

Across WO-PL-007 through WO-PL-016:

- 12 counted denials; zero successful out-of-grant mutations;
- 57 same-work-order drift items and eight distinct later-detected instances,
  represented by ten row attributions because D.7.1 records an instance in
  both its discovering and originating work-order rows;
- 19 rework cycles, 18 additional sessions, and seven retrieved artifacts;
- five of ten activation corpora measured; every measured cycle had zero
  routing gaps and zero orphan Tier-2 documents;
- zero whole surfaces qualified as mechanically enforced under the strict
  complete-channel metric in every counted order;
- eight reported Owner briefs averaged 307 words and all stayed at or below
  the 500-word ceiling; two were not reported;
- no experimental instrument qualified under the adopted 9.2.8 definition;
- no closed artifact was retrieved to reconstruct intent.

The first five orders required four rework cycles; the last five required 15.
Different technical scope is a real confound, but the record does not support a
cost-reduction claim. The 9.2.4 prediction of one rework cycle is **falsified**;
its no-archaeology half is supported.

## Accepted adverse findings

1. **Manifest regression.** WO-PL-014, WO-PL-015, and WO-PL-016 omitted the
   Appendix B `enforced_by` and `unenforced_boundaries` fields, and the new
   validator standardized rather than caught the omission. Closed work orders
   remain unchanged evidence. WO-PL-017 repairs the future-facing checker.
2. **Shell-rule departure.** The signed pilot disposition said
   `shell.execute` defaulted to denied; nine of ten counted orders declared it
   restricted. This is a historical operating departure, not repaired
   retroactively. Future orders state their actual provider envelope and its
   instruction-only boundaries before dispatch.
3. **Empty-domain stop rule.** The pilot pause rule for a successful mutation
   through a surface represented as mechanically enforced could not fire while
   every per-order strict surface count was zero. Future experiments define
   stop conditions over declared, birth-tested channels as well as whole
   surfaces. The completed pilot's rule is not rewritten after the fact.
4. **Routing materialization.** WO-PL-014 granted an Implementer write access to
   the ratified routing map and did not separate proposed wording from Owner
   ratification. The content of R.12, R.13, and R.14 is ratified now; the prior
   sequence remains a recorded deviation.
5. **Derived-record drift.** The WO-PL-014 later-detection count and current
   State were incomplete or stale. WO-PL-017 corrects current derived records
   without editing closed evidence.
6. **Reviewer independence.** The final two pilot orders relied on Codex to run
   gates, drive corrections, and state conformance. Their deterministic results
   remain reproducible; their independence claim is qualified. This fresh Opus
   evaluation supplies a separate post-pilot judgment, not a retroactive
   replacement for the missing separation.

## Prospective operating rules

These rules govern Plumbline work after this decision; they do not alter pilot
measurements retroactively.

1. Every work order includes a complete Appendix B classification. Every
   declared grant surface appears exactly once in `enforced_by` or
   `unenforced_boundaries`. The dispatch validator fails closed on omission,
   wrong type, undeclared classification, overlap, or incomplete coverage.
2. Owner active minutes are invited at closeout and recorded exactly as
   self-reported. If the Owner does not report them, the record says
   `NOT REPORTED`; no estimate is inferred.
3. A Reviewer distinct from the author of the correction must inspect final
   bytes. If that separation is unavailable, the deviation is explicit and the
   conformance confidence cannot be HIGH.
4. Agents may draft proposed routing text only under an explicit grant. Only
   exact wording already ratified by the Owner may be materialized in the
   routing map by the Owner or an explicitly authorized transcriber. Delegating
   keystrokes does not delegate authority.
5. Deterministic-checker disagreements with an implementation claim continue
   to count as drift under the adopted pilot definitions. A future methodology
   revision may define a prospective instrument metric; WO-PL-017 does not
   rewrite 9.2.8 after measurement.

## Publication claims

Plumbline may truthfully describe the bounded counts above, its zero successful
out-of-grant mutations, its strong same-cycle detection ratio, its absence of
intent-reconstruction archaeology, and its practice of preserving failed and
missing measurements. It may not claim empirical proof, reduced operating cost,
complete mechanical containment, universal Reviewer independence, or evidence
about another repository.
