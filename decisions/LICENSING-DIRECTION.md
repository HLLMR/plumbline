This file records the Owner's present direction on licensing. It is **not** a decision record, it ratifies nothing, and it may change without amendment. It exists so the direction lives in the repository rather than in a conversation (Doctrine 7.9.4).

**Status: deferred to the publication decision.** No license has been chosen and none has been added.

## Present direction, nonbinding

| Scope | Present direction |
|---|---|
| The doctrine and explanatory documentation | CC BY 4.0 |
| Executable adapters, scripts, checks, and tooling | Apache-2.0 |
| Templates and mixed-content skill bundles | Explicit later treatment required; not settled by the two entries above |
| Top-level license-scope map | To be written exactly, path by path, before publication |

The templates and the skill bundle are called out separately because they are genuinely mixed: a template is documentation that becomes a project's operating file, and the adoption bundle carries prose and executable code in one directory. Assigning them by analogy to either row above would be a guess, and a licensing guess is the kind that surfaces years later.

## What the absence of a license does and does not block

**Does not block:** private ratification, which is complete (`DR-001`, 2026-08-16), and repository self-adoption, which is also complete (the signed `governance/decisions/DR-001.md` and the adoption commit of 2026-08-17; see `SELF-HOSTING.md`). Both are private acts inside a private repository, and both were performed with no license chosen — which is exactly what this row predicted.

**Does block:** public release. Distributing a methodology with no license terms is a decision by default, and the wrong one. Nothing in this repository may be made public until the scope map above is written, the licenses are chosen, and the choice is recorded as a decision record rather than as direction.
