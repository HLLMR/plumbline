## Decision

Ratify Plumbline Doctrine revision 0.6 as the first authoritative revision of the methodology. Revision 0.1 was a proposal only and is not a prior ratified revision.

## Reasoning

Revision 0.6 establishes a coherent, document-controlled system connecting human-ratified intent, canonical project records, bounded work orders, capability grants, routed context, independent review, truthful enforcement claims, and measured outcomes.

Pre-ratification review resolved the known internal contradictions and distribution defects. Deterministic checks now protect the relationships among the Doctrine, extracted templates, bundled adoption materials, reference adapter, and release package.

Ratification does not represent the methodology as empirically proven, complete, or final. It establishes the stable authoritative baseline required to conduct governed pilots, collect real examples, measure failures and recovery costs under Part 9, and revise the methodology from evidence.

## Rejected alternatives

1. Continue holding v0.6 as a candidate until examples exist. Rejected because counted adoption and pilot evidence require an authoritative revision; waiting would make the evidence gate circular.
2. Treat v0.1 as ratified. Rejected because it was proposed only, and doing otherwise would misrepresent the historical authority boundary.
3. Delay ratification until a complete platform, validator, or multi-provider enforcement system exists. Rejected because Plumbline is a methodology rather than a platform, and supporting tooling should grow from observed need.
4. Ratify while the self-hosting model remains contradictory. Rejected because Plumbline must be capable of following the rules it publishes.
5. Treat v0.6 as final. Rejected because revision from collected pilot evidence is an intended part of the methodology.

## Determination concerning revision 0.1

Recorded here because it establishes the numbering of this record and the authority boundary behind it. The Owner determines conclusively that:

- v0.1 was proposed only;
- v0.1 was never ratified;
- its ratification-like heading and metadata were premature and inaccurate;
- it never carried authority over any project;
- revision 0.6 is therefore the first ratified Plumbline revision, and this record is `DR-001`.
