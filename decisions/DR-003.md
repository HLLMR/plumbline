an extracted file under `templates/**` under CC0-1.0. This is an intentional
license assignment by the copyright holder, not a conflict.

## Names and revision designations

The Plumbline name, Plumbline revision designations, and associated marks are
not licensed by the licenses above. A factual statement that an independent
work conforms with a named Plumbline Doctrine revision is not prohibited by
this reservation.

## Boundaries

This decision selects licenses and records ownership. It does not:

- publish the repository, change its visibility, push, tag, or release it;
- register a trademark or decide patent posture;
- reproduce or replace the executed copyright assignment;
- amend the Doctrine or ratify a new Doctrine revision; or
- license the Plumbline name, revision designations, or associated marks.

The detailed human-readable maps, canonical legal texts, contribution terms,
and machine-checkable metadata are implemented only through separately
governed work orders. Until those gates pass and the Owner separately decides
to publish, this repository remains private and unreleased.
